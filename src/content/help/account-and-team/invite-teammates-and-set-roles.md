Everyone on your team sees the same domains and results. Roles decide what each person can change.

## Roles

| Role | What they can do |
| --- | --- |
| Owner | Everything, including billing. There is one Owner per team, the person who created it. |
| Admin | Add and remove domains, change scan settings, invite people and manage the team, rename the team. Receives email alerts. |
| Member | Find and monitor pages, add URLs and run scans. |
| Viewer | See every domain, report and history. Cannot change anything. |

## Invite Someone

Admins and the Owner can invite people.

1. Go to **Team** in the sidebar.
2. Under **Invite a teammate**, enter their email address.
3. Choose a role. Only the Owner can make someone an Admin.
4. Select **Send invite**.

If the person is new to AccessBell, they receive an email with a link to set a password and join. If they already have an AccessBell account, they are added straight away.

## Change a Role

In **Team**, choose a new role from the menu next to the person. You can only change the role of people below your own level, and the Owner's role cannot be changed.

## Remove Someone

Select **Remove** next to the person. Admins can remove Members and Viewers. The Owner can remove anyone except themselves. Their access ends straight away.

## Leave a Team

Select **Leave team** next to your own name. The Owner cannot leave. To hand a team over, [contact support](/contact?topic=support).
