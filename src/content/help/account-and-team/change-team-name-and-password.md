## Rename Your Team

Admins and the Owner can rename the team. The name appears at the top of the sidebar for everyone on the team.

1. Select **Account settings** in the sidebar.
2. Under **Team name**, type the new name, up to 120 characters.
3. Select **Save**.

## Change Your Password

1. Select **Account settings**.
2. Under **Change password**, enter a new password of at least 8 characters.
3. Select **Update password**.

Choose a password you do not use anywhere else. A password manager makes this easy.

## Reset a Forgotten Password

1. On the [sign-in page](/app/login), select **Forgot password?**
2. Enter your email address and select the button to send the link.
3. Open the email and follow the link. It takes you to a page where you set a new password, then to your dashboard.

For your security, the same message appears whether or not an account exists for that address. If no email arrives, check your spam folder, then see [I did not get the confirmation email](/resources/help-center/troubleshooting/confirmation-email-not-received).
