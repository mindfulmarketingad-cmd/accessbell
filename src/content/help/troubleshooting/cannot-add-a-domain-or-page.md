## The Add Domain Button Is Missing

Only Admins and the Owner can add domains. Ask one of them, or ask the Owner to change your role. See [Invite teammates and set roles](/resources/help-center/account-and-team/invite-teammates-and-set-roles).

## "Start your 3-day free trial to use this feature"

Your team does not have an active subscription yet, or it has ended. The Owner can start or restart it from **Billing**. If the message says **Your last payment failed**, see [Failed payments](/resources/help-center/billing/failed-payments).

## "Your plan covers 1 domain. Add another domain in Billing to monitor more."

Every domain slot in your plan is taken. The number in the message is how many your plan includes. Remove a domain you no longer need, or [add more domains to your plan](/resources/help-center/billing/add-more-domains-to-your-plan).

## "That page is not on example.com"

Pages must be on the domain's own address. To monitor `blog.example.com` under `example.com`, turn on **Include subdomains** in the domain's Settings. Otherwise, add it as a separate domain.

## "Pro monitors up to 500 URLs per domain"

The domain already monitors 500 URLs. Stop monitoring a page you need less, or [talk to us](/contact?topic=sales) about a larger limit.

## "example.com is already in your account"

The domain has already been added. Find it under Domains in the sidebar.

## The Monitor and Scan Now Buttons Are Missing

Viewers cannot change pages or run scans, and neither can anyone while the subscription is inactive. Check your role in **Team** and your status in **Billing**.
