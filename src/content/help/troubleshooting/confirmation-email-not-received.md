Emails usually arrive within a minute. If yours has not:

## 1. Check Other Folders

Look in spam, junk, promotions and updates. In Gmail, search for **AccessBell**. If you find it there, mark it as not spam so future emails arrive in your inbox.

## 2. Resend It

- **Confirmation email**: on the "Check your email" screen, or on the sign-in page after trying to sign in, select **Resend confirmation email**. You can resend once a minute.
- **Password reset**: request it again from [Forgot password](/app/forgot).
- **Team invitation**: ask the person who invited you to remove and invite you again.

Links expire after a while, so always use the newest email.

## 3. Check the Address

Make sure there is no typo in the email address. If there is, sign up again with the correct address.

## 4. Company Email Filters

Some company email systems hold messages from new senders. Ask your IT team to allow email from accessbell.co, or try a different address.

## Still Nothing?

[Contact support](/contact?topic=support) with the address you signed up with, and we will help you get in.
