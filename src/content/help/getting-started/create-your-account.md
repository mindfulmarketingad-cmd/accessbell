You need an account to monitor domains, save scan history and invite your team.

## Sign Up

1. Go to [Create your account](/app/signup).
2. Enter your work email and a password of at least 8 characters.
3. Select **Create account**.

We send a confirmation link to your email address. Open it to finish creating your account. The link signs you in and takes you to your dashboard.

## If the Email Does Not Arrive

- Wait a few minutes, then check your spam, junk or promotions folder.
- On the "Check your email" screen, select **Resend confirmation email**. You can resend once a minute.
- If you try to sign in before confirming, the sign-in page shows a **Resend confirmation email** button too.

Still nothing? See [I did not get the confirmation email](/resources/help-center/troubleshooting/confirmation-email-not-received).

## What Happens Next

Your account starts with a team named after your email domain, with you as the **Owner**. You can rename the team in [Account settings](/resources/help-center/account-and-team/change-team-name-and-password).

To add domains, [start your free trial](/resources/help-center/getting-started/start-your-free-trial).
