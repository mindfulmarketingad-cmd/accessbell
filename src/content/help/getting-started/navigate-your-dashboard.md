The dashboard has a sidebar on the left and your work on the right. On phones and small tablets, open the sidebar with the menu button at the top left.

## The Sidebar

- **Workspace** at the top shows your team name and how many members it has.
- **Domains** opens Your Domains. Underneath is a list of every domain, so you can jump straight to one, plus **Add domain**. Use the arrow next to Domains to show or hide the list.
- **Team** is where you invite people and set roles.
- **Billing** shows your plan, trial and renewal dates, and opens the Stripe portal.
- **Account settings** is where you rename the team and change your password.
- **Support** takes you to our contact form.
- Your name and email are at the bottom. The menu button next to them has Account settings, Billing and **Sign out**.

On desktop, the button next to the logo collapses the sidebar to icons only. Your choice is remembered on that browser.

## Your Domains

The home page of the dashboard. It shows a count of your domains, summary numbers across all of them, and a card for each domain with its score, open issues, monitored URLs and last scan date.

## Inside a Domain

The header shows the domain name, when it was last scanned, **Re-Scan**, **Export** and a menu with **Find pages**, **Domain settings** and **Open website**. Below are five tabs:

| Tab | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| Overview | Last scan summary, score, scan history chart, WCAG coverage table and component grouping |
| Issues | Every failing rule with where it happens and how to fix it |
| Manual Review | Items automated testing could not decide |
| Pages | Monitored URLs, discovered pages and adding URLs |
| Settings | WCAG target, devices, URL rules, custom headers and removing the domain |

Read more in [Understanding the domain overview](/resources/help-center/scans-and-reports/understanding-the-domain-overview).
