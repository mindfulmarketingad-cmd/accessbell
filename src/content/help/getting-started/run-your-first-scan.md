Once a domain is added, you can scan all of its monitored pages at once or one page at a time. Rescans are unlimited on the Pro plan.

## Scan Every Monitored Page

1. Open the domain from [Your Domains](/app) or the sidebar.
2. Select **Re-Scan** at the top right.
3. A progress line shows how many pages are done. Pages are scanned two at a time, and each one usually takes 10 to 30 seconds.

When the scan finishes, the overview, the issues list and the history chart update automatically.

## Scan One Page

1. Open the domain and go to the **Pages** tab.
2. Find the page under **Monitored URLs** and select **Rescan**.

## What Gets Scanned

Each page is scanned on every device you choose in the domain's **Settings** tab (desktop, mobile, or both), against the WCAG version and level set there. The default is WCAG 2.2 Level AA on desktop. See [Choose WCAG version, level and devices](/resources/help-center/domains/scan-settings).

## After the First Scan

- Read the [domain overview](/resources/help-center/scans-and-reports/understanding-the-domain-overview).
- Open the **Issues** tab to see [what to fix first](/resources/help-center/fixing-issues/read-an-issue).
- From now on, monitored pages are also scanned automatically every day. See [Scheduled monitoring and email alerts](/resources/help-center/scans-and-reports/scheduled-monitoring-and-alerts).

If a page cannot be scanned, see [A page could not be scanned](/resources/help-center/troubleshooting/page-could-not-be-scanned).
