An accessibility statement tells visitors how accessible your website is, the standard you work to and how to report a barrier. The W3C recommends publishing one, and it shows your commitment to customers. AccessBell writes it for you from a few answers.

## Start the Statement

- During **Add Domain**, on the **Accessibility statement** step, select **Create statement**.
- For any domain, go to **Settings** > **Accessibility statement** and select **Create statement** or **Edit statement**.

## The Six Steps

1. **Organization type**: business, nonprofit, government agency, school or other.
2. **Company name**, and how accessible the site is today: partially conformant or fully conformant. Only choose fully conformant if you have tested the site and are confident it meets the standard.
3. **Contact info**: the email address, and optionally the phone number, people can use to report a barrier or ask for another format.
4. **Related policies & links** (optional): up to 5 links, such as your privacy policy or a feedback form.
5. **Custom notes** (optional): for example, known limitations and when you plan to fix them, or alternatives you offer.
6. **Install statement**: your statement is saved. Preview it and choose how to publish it.

The statement names the WCAG version and level from your domain's settings.

## Publish It

- **Hosted link**: select **Copy link** and link to it from your site footer, for example with the text "Accessibility". The hosted page stays up to date, including the date your site was last checked.
- **On your own site**: select **Copy HTML** or **Download HTML** and paste it into a new page on your site.

Want a statement without an account? Use the free [accessibility statement generator](/tools/statement-generator).

## Who Can Do This

Admins and the Owner can create and edit the statement.
