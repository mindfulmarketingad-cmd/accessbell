Share results with people who do not use AccessBell, keep records for audits, or plan work in a spreadsheet.

## Export Issues to Excel or Google Sheets

1. Open the domain.
2. Select **Export** at the top right, then **Issues as CSV (Excel)**.
3. Open the downloaded file in Excel, Numbers or Google Sheets.

The file has one row per failing rule, with:

- Issue name and severity
- WCAG criteria
- Number of failing elements and pages
- Affected page addresses
- How to fix it

It is named after the domain and today's date, for example `example.com-accessibility-issues-2026-09-28.csv`.

## Save a Report as a PDF

1. Open the domain.
2. Select **Export**, then **Print or save as PDF**.
3. In the print window, choose **Save as PDF** as the destination.

The printed report includes the overview, scan history, the coverage table and every issue with its fix, without the sidebar and buttons.

To print a single page's report instead, open it from the **Pages** tab and use your browser's print command.
