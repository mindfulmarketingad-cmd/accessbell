Every scan is saved, so you can show progress to your team, leadership or an auditor.

## Domain History Chart

On a domain's **Overview**, the **Scan history** chart shows one point per day for the last 7, 30 or 90 days. Choose the range from the menu at the top right of the chart.

- **Score**: the average score of pages scanned that day
- **Open issues**: failing elements found that day
- **Pages scanned**

Select a line's name to hide or show it. Point at the chart, or focus it and use the arrow keys, to see exact values for a day. Screen readers get the same data as a table.

## History for One Page

1. Open the domain and go to the **Pages** tab.
2. Select the date under **Last scan** for the page.

You see every scan of that page with its date, device, score, issue count, whether it was manual or scheduled, and the standard it used. Select a date to open that scan's full report.

## Open a Full Report

In the **Pages** tab, select a page's address to open its latest report. Reports include every issue with the failing code, manual review items and passed checks, and you can [filter them by WCAG version and level](/resources/help-center/scans-and-reports/filter-reports-by-wcag-version).

## Keeping Evidence

Scan history is kept for as long as the domain and page exist in your account. Removing a domain deletes its history, so [export](/resources/help-center/scans-and-reports/export-reports) anything you need first.
