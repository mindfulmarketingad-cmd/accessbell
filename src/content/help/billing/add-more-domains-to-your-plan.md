Each domain in your plan costs $79 per month. The number of domains decides how many you can add to your dashboard.

## Increase the Number of Domains

1. Go to **Billing** and select **Manage billing**. Only the Owner can do this.
2. In the Stripe portal, update the quantity on your subscription.
3. Confirm the change and return to AccessBell.

The new domain slots are ready as soon as Stripe confirms the change, usually within seconds. Stripe works out any prorated charge for the rest of the billing period.

## Reduce the Number of Domains

1. First [remove the domains](/resources/help-center/domains/remove-a-domain) you no longer need, so the number in use is not above the new number.
2. Then lower the quantity in the Stripe portal the same way.

If the plan ends up with fewer domains than you have added, you cannot add new ones until you remove some or raise the number again. Your existing domains and history are kept.
