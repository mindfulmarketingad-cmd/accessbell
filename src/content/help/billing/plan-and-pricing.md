AccessBell has one plan, **Pro**, at **$79 per domain per month**. Every feature is included on every domain.

## What Is Included

- Up to **25 monitored URLs per domain**
- **Unlimited rescans** and daily scheduled monitoring
- WCAG 2.0, 2.1 or 2.2 at Level A, AA or AAA
- Desktop and mobile testing, subdomains, staging sites with custom headers, page load delay and scrolling
- Automatic page discovery from your sitemap and links
- Domain overview, WCAG coverage table, issue details with code examples, manual review items, component grouping and scan history
- CSV export and printable reports
- Email alerts for new serious issues
- Team members with roles
- Email support

See the full list on the [pricing page](/pricing).

## What Counts as a URL

Each unique page address you monitor counts as one URL. Discovered pages you have not chosen to monitor do not count. Desktop and mobile scans of the same page count as one URL.

## Unlimited Rescans and Fair Use

Rescan as often as you like. To keep the service fast for everyone, very high automated volumes, such as hundreds of scans an hour from one account, may be slowed down.

## Price per Domain

Your monthly price is $79 multiplied by the number of domains in your plan. The **Billing** page shows the current total. See [Add more domains to your plan](/resources/help-center/billing/add-more-domains-to-your-plan).
