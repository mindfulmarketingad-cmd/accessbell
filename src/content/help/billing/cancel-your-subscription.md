You can cancel at any time. Only the account Owner can cancel.

## Cancel

1. Go to **Billing** and select **Manage billing**.
2. In the Stripe portal, select **Cancel subscription** and confirm.

**During the free trial**: cancel before the date shown under **First charge** and you will not be charged.

**After the trial**: your plan stays active until the end of the period you have paid for, shown under **Renews**. You are not charged again.

## What Happens After Cancelling

- Your account, domains and scan history are kept, so you can see past results.
- Scheduled monitoring stops, and you cannot run new scans, add domains or monitor pages.
- The dashboard shows a banner to restart the subscription.

## Restart Later

Go to **Billing** and select **Restart subscription**. You check out again with Stripe, and everything is ready as soon as payment is confirmed.

Want to delete your account and data completely? [Contact support](/contact?topic=support).
