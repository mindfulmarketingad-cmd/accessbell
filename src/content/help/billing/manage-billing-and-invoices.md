Payments are handled by Stripe. AccessBell never sees or stores your full card number. Only the account **Owner** can manage billing.

## The Billing Page

Select **Billing** in the sidebar to see:

- **Status**: Free trial, Active, Payment failed, Canceled and so on
- **Domains in plan**, and how many are in use
- **Monthly price**
- **First charge** during a trial, or **Renews** after it

## Open the Stripe Portal

Select **Manage billing**. You are taken to a secure Stripe page where you can:

- Update your card or payment method
- Download invoices and receipts
- Update your billing name, address and tax details
- Change the number of domains
- Cancel your subscription

When you are done, return to AccessBell from the link on the Stripe page. Changes appear on the Billing page within a few seconds.

Team members who are not the Owner see the Billing page but cannot open the portal.
