One AccessBell account can monitor as many domains as your plan includes. Each domain has its own 500 monitored URLs, settings, history and issues.

## How Domain Slots Work

Your plan has a number of domains, which you choose at checkout. Each one is $29 per month. The **Billing** page shows how many are in your plan and how many are in use, and **Add Domain** tells you when every slot is used.

Different subdomains are different domains. `example.com` and `shop.example.com` use two slots, unless you monitor the shop pages under `example.com` with **Include subdomains** turned on. A staging site added separately also uses a slot.

## Add More Domains to Your Plan

The account Owner can increase the number of domains at any time. See [Add more domains to your plan](/resources/help-center/billing/add-more-domains-to-your-plan). As soon as Stripe confirms the change, the new slots are ready in your dashboard.

## Compare Domains

[Your Domains](/app) shows every domain as a card with its score, open issues, monitored URLs and last scan date. Above the cards are totals across all domains: average score, open issues, monitored URLs and domain slots used.

Every domain also appears in the sidebar under **Domains**, so you can move between them in one click.

## Agencies and Teams

If you manage sites for clients, add each client site as a domain and [invite your team](/resources/help-center/account-and-team/invite-teammates-and-set-roles). Use the **Viewer** role for clients who should see results but not change anything. Viewers can see every domain in the account.
