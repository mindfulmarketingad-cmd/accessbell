Removing a domain deletes it, its pages and all of its scan history. This cannot be undone. Only Admins and the Owner can remove domains.

## Before You Remove It

If you may need the results later, [export them](/resources/help-center/scans-and-reports/export-reports) first.

## Remove the Domain

1. Open the domain and go to the **Settings** tab.
2. Scroll to **Remove domain** and select the button.
3. Confirm. You return to Your Domains.

The slot is free straight away, so you can add a different domain in its place.

## Lower Your Bill

Removing a domain does not change your subscription. To pay for fewer domains, the Owner can reduce the number in the Stripe portal. See [Add more domains to your plan](/resources/help-center/billing/add-more-domains-to-your-plan), which covers lowering the number too.
