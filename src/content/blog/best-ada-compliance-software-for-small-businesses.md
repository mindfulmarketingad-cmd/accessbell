**ADA compliance software** helps small businesses find and fix the website barriers that lead to accessibility lawsuits: missing alt text, unlabeled form fields, low contrast and buttons a keyboard cannot reach. The best ADA compliance software for small businesses does this continuously, shows you exactly what to fix and keeps a record you can point to if a demand letter arrives. We compared five popular options by features, pricing and free resources. [AccessBell](/) is our tool and our top pick, and we explain below where each of the others fits.

## Why Small Businesses Need ADA Compliance Software in 2026

Small businesses are the main target of website accessibility lawsuits. Plaintiffs filed **3,117 website accessibility lawsuits in U.S. federal court in 2025**, up 27% from 2024 ([Seyfarth Shaw](https://www.adatitleiii.com/2026/03/federal-court-website-accessibility-lawsuit-filings-bounce-back-in-2025/)), and UsableNet found that **64% of the companies sued in the first half of 2025 had annual revenue under $25 million** ([UsableNet](https://blog.usablenet.com/2025-midyear-accessibility-lawsuit-report-key-legal-trends)).

The Department of Justice says businesses open to the public must make their websites accessible to people with disabilities ([ADA.gov web guidance](https://www.ada.gov/resources/web-guidance/)). Courts and settlements usually measure that against the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA. ADA compliance software is how a small team without an accessibility specialist keeps up.

## How We Chose the Best ADA Compliance Software for Small Businesses

We looked for tools a small business can buy without a sales call and compared them on:

- **Does it fix your code, or patch the page?** Scanners show you what to change in your site. Overlays add a script that changes the page in the visitor's browser.
- **Ongoing monitoring:** scheduled scans and alerts, not a one-time report.
- **Help fixing issues:** the failing element, the WCAG criterion and a corrected example.
- **Evidence:** scan history and a record of fixes you can show a lawyer.
- **Price for one small site,** from the vendor's own pricing page where published.
- **Free resources:** scanners, guides and generators you can use before paying.

## 1. AccessBell: Best ADA Compliance Software for Small Businesses Overall

[AccessBell](/) is built for small and mid-sized businesses that want to fix their websites properly, at a price that does not change with traffic. It scans every page in a real Chrome browser with [axe-core](https://github.com/dequelabs/axe-core), the open-source testing engine used across the industry, and rescans on a schedule.

### AccessBell Features

- **Continuous monitoring** of up to 500 URLs per domain, with automatic page discovery, sitemap scanning, unlimited rescans and email alerts.
- **Your choice of standard:** WCAG 2.2, 2.1 or 2.0 at Level A, AA or AAA, with presets for the ADA, Section 508 and EN 301 549.
- **Code-level fixes:** each issue shows the failing HTML, the WCAG success criterion, a severity rating and a corrected example. Issues caused by a shared template are grouped, so one fix clears them all.
- **AccessBellFix:** an optional one-line script that applies fixes you approve, such as alt text and button names, while you update your code.
- **Compliance Vault:** dated scan records with a verifiable fingerprint, a fix log with remediation notes and exportable evidence packages. See [how the Compliance Vault works](/resources/help-center/scans-and-reports/compliance-vault).
- **PDF checks:** finds PDFs linked from your pages, checks them and fixes the title and language for you.
- **Accessibility statement** that updates from your scan results, plus manual testing procedures for what automation cannot check.
- **PageAssist toolbar:** an optional visitor toolbar for text size, contrast, spacing and motion. It helps visitors; it does not fix your code.
- **PDF and Excel exports,** team roles and staging-site scanning.

### AccessBell Use Case Examples

- **An online store adds new products every week.** Monitoring flags the new product photos that went up without alt text. The owner approves alt text in AccessBellFix the same day, and the fix is logged in the Compliance Vault.
- **A restaurant posts its menu as a PDF.** AccessBell finds the PDF on the menu page, reports that it has no title or language, fixes both and flags that it needs tags from the source file.
- **A dental practice receives a demand letter.** The office manager exports an evidence package showing months of scan history and the fixes made, and hands it to their lawyer.
- **A web agency looks after 12 client sites.** Each client gets its own domain, scans run automatically and the agency sends each client a monthly PDF report.

## 2. AccessibilityChecker.org: Best for AI-Assisted Fix Suggestions

AccessibilityChecker.org is a scanner, like AccessBell, not an overlay. It adds SmartFix, which suggests AI-written fixes for supported issues that you review before they go live, a generated accessibility statement and exportable audit records.

- **Pricing:** from $69 per month billed annually for up to 25 URLs, with larger plans for bigger sites.
- **Free resources:** a free scan and a 7-day free trial.
- **Watch out for:** the entry plan covers far fewer pages than AccessBell's 500 per domain, at more than twice the monthly price.

**Best for:** small sites that want AI-suggested fix text and are happy to pay more for it.

## 3. AudioEye: Best for Managed Services

AudioEye combines automated detection and automatic fixes, delivered through a script on your site, with paid human services such as expert audits and remediation. That managed layer is its strength if you would rather a vendor handle the work.

- **Pricing:** AudioEye sells several tiers. Software directories such as [Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/214283/Audioeye/) list entry plans from about $49 per month, and plans that include expert testing cost considerably more. Confirm current prices with AudioEye.
- **Free resources:** a free website scan and a large library of articles and webinars.
- **Watch out for:** its automatic fixes run in the visitor's browser, and accessibility professionals list AudioEye on the [Overlay Fact Sheet](https://overlayfactsheet.com/en/). Budget for the managed tiers if you want your code actually fixed.

**Best for:** businesses that want to hand remediation to a vendor and can pay for managed plans.

## 4. UserWay: Best Free Widget, With Big Caveats

UserWay is an accessibility overlay: a widget that changes how your page looks and behaves in the visitor's browser. It has a free version and paid AI-powered plans that add limited page monitoring and an accessibility statement.

- **Pricing:** a free widget, with paid plans from $490 per year, according to [Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/218549/UserWay/pricing/).
- **Free resources:** the free widget and a free site scan.
- **Watch out for:** a widget does not change your underlying code, and UserWay is named on the Overlay Fact Sheet. Read our [UserWay alternative](/comparisons/userway-vs-accessbell) breakdown before relying on it for compliance.

**Best for:** sites that want a free visitor toolbar alongside, not instead of, real fixes.

## 5. accessiBe: Best Known, and the Most Controversial

accessiBe's accessWidget is the best-known accessibility overlay. It uses AI to adjust your page in the visitor's browser and offers a visitor toolbar.

- **Pricing:** priced by monthly website visits, from $490 per year for up to 5,000 visits a month, rising to $1,490 and $3,990 per year for higher traffic ([accessiBe pricing](https://accessibe.com/pricing/accesswidget)).
- **Free resources:** a free scanner and published guides.
- **Watch out for:** the FTC ordered accessiBe to [pay $1 million](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2025/04/ftc-approves-final-order-requiring-accessibe-pay-1-million) over claims that its widget could make any website compliant. See our [accessiBe alternative](/comparisons/accessibe-vs-accessbell) guide.

**Best for:** we do not recommend relying on it for compliance. If you already use it, pair it with a scanner that shows you what to fix in your code.

## How to Choose ADA Compliance Software for Your Small Business

1. **Run a free scan first.** [Scan your site](/#scan) to see how many issues you are starting with.
2. **Choose a tool that changes your code.** Overlays can help some visitors, but lawsuits are about the barriers in your site. Our [ADA website compliance guide](/blog/ada-website-compliance-guide) explains what to fix first.
3. **Make sure it monitors, not just scans once.** Every new product, post or plugin can add a barrier.
4. **Keep evidence.** If you ever receive a demand letter, dated records of your testing and fixes matter. Read [what happens after being sued for ADA website compliance](/blog/what-happens-after-being-sued-for-ada-website-compliance).
5. **Check the price at your size.** Traffic-based pricing can climb as you grow; per-domain pricing stays predictable.
