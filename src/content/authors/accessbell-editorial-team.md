The AccessBell editorial team researches and writes the guides on this blog. Our goal is simple: give website owners, designers and facility managers accurate, practical answers they can act on.

## How we write

Every article is checked against the primary source, such as the W3C's Web Content Accessibility Guidelines, the 2010 ADA Standards for Accessible Design or guidance from the U.S. Department of Justice. Where a requirement has a section number, we cite it so you can verify it yourself.

We update articles when standards, deadlines or guidance change, and each article shows a review history so you can see what changed and when.

## What we do not do

We do not provide legal advice. Our guides explain requirements in plain language, but your obligations depend on your situation and local codes. For building projects, confirm details with your local code official or an accessibility specialist.
