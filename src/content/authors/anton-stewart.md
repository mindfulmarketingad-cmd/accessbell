Anton Stewart contributes to AccessBell's guides on web accessibility, WCAG and accessibility testing.

Every article Anton Stewart works on follows our editorial process: requirements are checked against the primary source, such as the W3C's [Web Content Accessibility Guidelines](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/), and each article shows a review history so you can see what changed and when.

Spotted an error in one of our guides? [Tell us](/contact) and we will review it.
