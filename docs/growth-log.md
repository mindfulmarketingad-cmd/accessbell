# Growth log

A running record of shipped pages and conversion changes. One page and one site improvement per entry.

## 2026-11-04

**New page:** `/industries/dental-practices` — Dental practice accessibility checker. Dental offices look for appointment and new-patient form fixes, and that page did not exist. It cites the ADA definition of a professional office of a health care provider in 42 U.S.C. § 12181(7)(F) and ADA.gov web guidance (which names hospitals and medical offices), then offers a free scan, the 3-day Pro trial, the $19 Page Report and the $79 Domain Audit.

**Site improvement:** The pricing page now states the trial next to the price. Pro is $29 per domain per month or $199 per year after a 3-day free trial. A card is added at checkout, and there is no charge until the trial ends. Canceling before then means no charge. The 14-day money-back guarantee stays on the first charge. People who do not want a subscription see the $19 Page Report and the $79 Domain Audit beside the trial button.

**Why:** The price said “billed monthly” and led with the 14-day money-back guarantee, so the 3-day no-charge trial was easy to miss, and the one-time reports sat below the full feature list. Dental offices are a Title III audience the industry hub did not cover.
