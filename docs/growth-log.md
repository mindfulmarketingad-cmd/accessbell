# Growth log

A running record of shipped pages and conversion changes. One page and one site improvement per entry.

## 2026-11-02

**New page:** `/industries/restaurants` — Restaurant accessibility checker. Restaurant owners search for menu, online-ordering and reservation fixes, and that page did not exist. It cites the ADA’s public-accommodation definition, ADA.gov’s web guidance and *Robles v. Domino’s Pizza*, then offers a free scan, the 3-day Pro trial, the $19 Page Report and the $79 Domain Audit.

**Site improvement:** After a free scan, the primary next step is the 3-day Pro trial on the signup page. The “whole website” prompt and the lawsuit-risk button no longer send people to the long pricing page first. Critical and serious issues link to the $19 Page Report with the scanned URL filled in. The $79 Domain Audit stays one click away.

**Why:** MRR is one Pro domain ($29). The highest-intent moment is the scan result, and the old buttons added a pricing-page detour before a trial or a one-time report. Restaurants are a Title III audience the industry hub did not cover.
